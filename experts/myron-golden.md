# Myron Golden

> A summary of this person's public teachings, written in our own words. It is not written or endorsed by them. Check the sources before you quote anything.

## Who they are

Myron Golden is a business coach, sales trainer, author and speaker based in Tampa, Florida. His book *From the Trash Man to the Cash Man* tells how he went from hauling trash to building wealth. He sold insurance and investments, lost a lot in the 2008 crash, and later rebuilt through online offers and selling from the stage. He runs the Make More Offers Challenge, hosts the B.O.S.S. Moves Podcast and a large YouTube channel, and is a frequent guest on The Russell Brunson Show (Brunson co-founded ClickFunnels). His teaching openly draws on his Christian faith and the Bible.

## Use this expert when

- Your sales are flat and you are not sure if the problem is the offer
- You want to build a high ticket offer (a higher price offer with more value and more access)
- You want a clear path of offers: a small first step, a main offer, a premium offer and a monthly offer
- You plan to sell to a group: a webinar, a workshop, a talk on a stage
- You feel stuck trading hours for dollars and want to see the next level up
- Money fear or self doubt is holding back your selling

## Core frameworks

### The Four Levels of Value

- **What it is:** Income follows the value you create. There are four levels. Each one pays more, and each uses a different resource.
  - **Implementation:** you do the work yourself. Resource: your muscle (your time and effort).
  - **Unification:** you lead and manage the people who do the work. Resource: management skill.
  - **Communication:** you use words to move people, like selling, teaching and speaking. Resource: your mouth.
  - **Imagination:** you create ideas, offers and systems that serve many people. Resources: your mind and your money.
- **How to use it:**
  1. Write down where your hours go this week.
  2. Mark each task by level.
  3. Move one lower level task off your plate (hire, automate or drop it).
  4. Spend the freed time on communication or imagination work, like making offers.
- **Source:** Russell Brunson Show episode "The Four Levels of Value" (clip of Golden teaching). Also retold by Stephen Diaz, who names Golden as one of his coaches, on the HoneyBook blog.

### The Four B.O.S.S. Moves

- **What it is:** B.O.S.S. stands for Business Optimization Success Secrets. Every business grows through four moves:
  - **Lead generation:** get the attention of people who may buy
  - **Lead conversion:** turn those people into buyers
  - **Customer ascension:** help buyers move up to bigger offers over time
  - **Customer retention:** keep buyers around and buying
- **How to use it:**
  1. Score each move from 1 to 10 for your business.
  2. Pick the weakest one.
  3. Make one change there this month before you touch the others.
- **Source:** *B.O.S.S. Moves* book (official book page). Note: some summaries list "recurring revenue" in place of one of these, so check the book itself.

### The four-offer path (Make More Offers Challenge)

- **What it is:** A stack of four offers so every buyer has a next step.
  - **Lead offer:** low cost or free, made to attract the right buyers
  - **Core offer:** your main paid product or service
  - **Premium offer:** a high ticket version with more access or more done for them
  - **Continuity offer:** a monthly offer, like a membership or retainer
- **How to use it:**
  1. List what you sell today in these four slots.
  2. Find the empty slot. That is often the missing money.
  3. Write the offer that fills it, then make it to your current buyers first.
- **Source:** Make More Offers Challenge, as described in an April 2026 paid-customer review (third party, not Golden's own words). The review calls them Irresistible Lead Offer, Core Product Offer, Premium Value Offer and Continuity Offer.

### Wealth has a need for speed

- **What it is:** Wealth is not only how much you earn but how fast. Earn the same money in less time and you grow faster.
- **How to use it:**
  1. **Raise the price.** He gives the example of taking an offer from $2,500 to $25,000: ten times the money in the same time.
  2. **Sell to many at once.** One talk or webinar to a group, not one call at a time.
  3. **Collapse timeframes.** Make the number of offers you now make in a month, in a week instead.
  4. **Sell a product, not only hours.** A product can sell to many people with little extra time.
- **Source:** Russell Brunson Show, "Wealth Has a Need for Speed" (guest Myron Golden).

### Be, Do, Have

- **What it is:** Who you are (be) drives what you do (do), and what you do brings what you get (have). Most frustration comes from gaps between these.
- **How to use it:**
  1. Name the result you want (have).
  2. Name the actions that would get it (do).
  3. Name the kind of person who does those actions without a fight (be).
  4. Close the gaps on purpose. He also says to picture the cost of not changing, not only the reward.
- **Source:** The Journey to Freedom Podcast, "Breaking Free from Your Lie-dentity."

## Key principles

- Business is solving other people's problems for a profit.
- If money is short, look at your offers first. Make better offers, and make them more often.
- Sell to people who want what you offer and can afford it, not to people who only need it and cannot pay.
- Higher prices can mean more sales, not fewer, when the value is clearly much bigger than the price.
- Persuade by serving the buyer, not by talking them into something.
- Growth needs mindset, skillset and toolset together.

## Mindset

- He went about 18 months without a sale early on. He treats failure as tuition, not a verdict.
- Identity first: change who you believe you are, and your actions and results follow.
- Wanting wealth is not wrong. He frames money as the reward for serving many people well.
- You do not need a perfect product to start. A real sale pushes you to finish and improve it.
- Speed matters. Move fast, make the offer, learn from the result.
- He tests ideas against his faith and the Bible, and he says so openly.

## Strategies and plays

- **Fill the empty offer slot.** Example: a coach with only a $500 program adds a $5,000 premium tier with group calls.
- **Raise the price and add value.** Example: double the price and add a done-with-you setup session.
- **Make offers more often.** Example: pitch your workshop every week instead of once a quarter.
- **Go one to many.** Example: run a free 45 minute webinar that ends with an offer, instead of ten 1 on 1 calls.
- **Climb the value levels.** Example: a dog walker hires two walkers (unification), then spends her time selling and building packages (communication and imagination).
- **Ascend your buyers.** Example: send every new client an invite to the next offer within 30 days.

## Questions they would ask you

Use these to pressure test a plan.

1. How many offers did you make last week? Not posts. Offers.
2. Which of your four offer slots is empty: lead, core, premium or monthly?
3. Are you selling to people who need this, or to people who want it and can pay for it?
4. What is this worth to the buyer, and is that far more than your price?
5. What would it take to sell this at ten times the price?
6. Which level of value are you spending most of your hours on?
7. How could you make this same offer to 100 people at once?
8. What happens to buyers after they buy? What is their next step up?
9. Who do you need to become to do what this plan asks?

## Watch out

- **Most of what is public is marketing.** Press releases and sales pages for his challenge include big income claims from students. These are not checked by anyone else. Do not treat them as typical.
- **Price advice can be misused.** "Raise your price" only works when the value truly grows too. A higher price on the same thin offer just loses buyers and trust.
- **"Make more offers" is not "pitch nonstop."** Too many offers to the same small list can wear people out. Serve first, then offer.
- **Local service businesses may not fit the high ticket model.** A dog walker or a cleaner may be better served by a monthly plan than a $10,000 package.
- **Selling to people who can pay is not the same as ignoring others.** Be clear about who your offer is for without talking down to anyone.
- **Faith is part of his teaching.** Some readers will welcome it and some will not. You can use the business ideas either way.
- **Sources disagree on details.** Lists for the four B.O.S.S. Moves vary a little across summaries. When it matters, check the book.
- **Never let this lens change a price or promise in `company/` without the founder's OK.**
